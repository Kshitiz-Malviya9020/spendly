Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide our expense tracking services, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your email address and password used for authentication.
  • Financial Data: Expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions that you manually enter into the app.
  • Usage Data: Basic information about how you interact with the application to help us improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data solely to provide and improve Spendly's services, including:

  • Generating your spending summaries and analytics.
  • Managing your user account and security.
  • Providing customer support.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access. You have full control over your data and can delete your transactions or account at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable or financial information to outside parties. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics that compromise your privacy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
